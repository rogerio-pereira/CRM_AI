# Disclaimer — Email marketing

**Service:** Email marketing  
**Public URL:** `/services/email-marketing`

## What this service is

Email marketing helps small businesses stay in touch in a human way: welcome notes, kind follow-ups, and useful updates so customers remember you between visits. Work may include mapping key moments, drafting copy in a friendly voice, setting up a simple send system, and refining based on what people open and respond to.

## What this page does not promise

- **No guaranteed open rates, click rates, reply rates, or revenue.** Exact numbers depend on your list quality, offer, timing, and how often you send.
- **No “miracle metrics.”** We will not invent or promise inflated open-rate theater. Email works when the relationship is real and the message is useful.
- **A small list of real customers is not a failure.** Quality beats volume; results still vary.
- **Submitting a contact form or booking a discovery call does not create a client engagement.** Paid work is governed by a separate written agreement.

## Scope boundaries

- You review and approve copy before anything sends.
- We may use AI to draft faster; messages are edited so they still feel like you, not a bulk blast. Nothing sends without your approval.
- Compliance with email laws (for example consent, unsubscribe, and accurate sender identity) remains a shared responsibility: we design sensible flows; you must have a lawful basis to contact the people on your list.
- Platform or ESP fees (if any) are separate from our fees unless a written agreement says otherwise.
