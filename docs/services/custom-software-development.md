# Disclaimer — Custom software development

**Service:** Custom software development  
**Public URL:** `/services/custom-software-development`  
**Last reviewed:** August 11, 2026

## What this service is

Custom software is focused tooling for small businesses when ready-made apps fight your process—portals, internal dashboards, booking flows, inventory helpers, and similar systems—without enterprise bloat. Work starts with discovery and honest scoping (“should we build?”), then a written plan, visible build slices, launch, and support as agreed.

## What this page does not promise

- **Custom is not always the answer.** If a simpler product, automation, or process change will do, we will say so—even when that is not the bigger invoice.
- **No guaranteed business outcomes** (revenue, efficiency %, or adoption) beyond delivering the scoped software to the agreed acceptance criteria.
- **Scope and cost can change** if requirements change. What is in and out of a phase is defined in writing; work outside that scope is new work.
- **Submitting a contact form or booking a discovery call does not create a client engagement.** Paid work is governed by a separate written agreement (and an NDA if you ask).

## Scope boundaries

- During development we typically host a private GitHub repository under our control. Confidential business details stay with you; we may keep a non-sensitive portfolio copy later as agreed.
- We may use AI to assist development. Architecture, code, tests, and business decisions are human-reviewed before they ship.
- Critical bugs during an agreed warranty window are on us; afterward, fixes and new features are scoped as new work.
- Hosting, third-party APIs, licenses, and ongoing ops may be separate costs. Security is built in from the start; special compliance regimes (if any) must be discussed early and may expand scope.
- Handover includes a working application plus source, docs, and repo/config access as defined in the agreement—not unbounded lifetime support unless contracted.
