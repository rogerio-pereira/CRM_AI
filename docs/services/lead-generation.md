# Disclaimer — Lead generation

**Service:** Lead generation  
**Public URL:** `/services/lead-generation`  
**Last reviewed:** August 11, 2026

## What this service is

Lead generation helps small businesses get more of the *right* people to reach out—calls, form fills, or booked conversations—not vanity clicks or empty traffic. Work may include clarifying the offer, landing pages or path-to-contact improvements, paid advertising setup and monitoring when ads are part of the plan, follow-up paths, and plain-language reporting on what brings inquiries.

## What this page does not promise

- **No guaranteed lead volume, revenue, or ROI.** Outcomes depend on your industry, offer, budget, market, and how quickly you respond to inquiries.
- **No guaranteed rankings, click-through rates, or cost-per-lead.** Past examples, testimonials, and case studies on the site are illustrative only.
- **Paid ads are not mandatory for every client**, and organic reach alone is not a free shortcut to steady inquiries. If ads (or lead generation as a whole) are the wrong first step, we will say so on a discovery call.
- **Submitting a contact form or booking a discovery call does not create a client engagement.** Paid work is governed by a separate written agreement.

## Scope boundaries

- We may use AI to draft headlines, ad variations, or landing-page ideas and to help interpret performance. A human still sets strategy, reviews what goes live, and decides where budget goes.
- Keeping your current website is often fine; we improve the path from attention to inquiry. If the site itself is holding leads back, we will say so honestly.
- Ad spend (media cost) is typically paid to the advertising platforms and is separate from our fees, unless a written agreement says otherwise.
