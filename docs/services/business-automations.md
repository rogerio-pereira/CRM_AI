**Service:** Business automations  
**Public URL:** `/services/business-automations`

## What this service is

Business automations connect the tools you already use (and sometimes a small integrator) so repetitive work—quotes, follow-ups, reminders, handoffs, alerts, and similar flows—runs without you chasing every detail. We map friction, design the simplest useful flow, connect and test, then hand over with a clear picture of what is automatic and what still needs a human.

## What this page does not promise

- **No guaranteed hours saved, lead conversion, or revenue.** Time and error reductions depend on your starting process, tool limits, and whether the team uses the new flow.
- **We will not automate chaos.** If a process is unclear, we tidy the thinking first. Automating a mess only makes a faster mess.
- **No promise of a sprawling “fully automated business.”** One or two high-value automations that you maintain beat a complex setup nobody finishes.
- **Submitting a contact form or booking a discovery call does not create a client engagement.** Paid work is governed by a separate written agreement.

## Scope boundaries

- New software is not always required; when it is, we explain cost and why before anything changes. Vendor/subscription fees are usually yours unless stated otherwise.
- Third-party APIs, rate limits, outages, and pricing changes can break or change automations; ongoing monitoring and fixes may be separate work after handoff unless included in writing.
- We may use AI inside a workflow only where it saves real time and stays under your control (for example drafting a first reply you still approve). Simple rule-based flows remain the default when they are enough.
- You remain responsible for the accuracy of business data and for decisions that automations surface or trigger.
