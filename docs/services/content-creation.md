# Disclaimer — Content creation

**Service:** Content creation  
**Public URL:** `/services/content-creation`

## What this service is

Content creation means **written** blog posts for your site and posts/captions for the social platforms you already use—clear, friendly, and consistent so customers keep hearing from you. We agree on topics and a sustainable cadence, draft and revise with your review, then publish on schedule.

## What this page does not promise

- **No guaranteed rankings, reach, engagement, or leads.** Thoughtful posts can support search and visibility over time; they are not an overnight ranking switch.
- **No content-mill volume or fake expertise.** We write from what you tell us about the business. If a blog or social cadence will not help right now, we will say so.
- **Consistency beats daily posting.** Cadence is agreed in writing; we do not promise virality or algorithm outcomes on any platform.
- **Submitting a contact form or booking a discovery call does not create a client engagement.** Paid work is governed by a separate written agreement.

## Scope boundaries

- **We do not take photos or record videos.** You provide images when a post needs them, or we write text-first posts that work without custom photography.
- Nothing goes live until you approve it.
- We may use AI as a drafting aid. Every piece still gets human editing for voice, accuracy, and whether it sounds like your business.
- Platform algorithm changes, account suspensions, and third-party posting tools are outside our control.
- Ads, website builds, and email sequences are separate services unless the agreement ties them together.
