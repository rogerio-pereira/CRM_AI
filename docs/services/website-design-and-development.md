## What this service is

We design and build websites for small businesses that need more than a pretty brochure: clear pages, mobile-friendly experience, fast loads, and an obvious next step (call, book, or message). Work typically moves through discovery, structure and design, build and refine, then launch and handoff with a walkthrough of the basics.

## What this page does not promise

- **No guaranteed leads, rankings, or revenue from a new or refreshed site alone.** A site supports growth; results still depend on offer, traffic sources, follow-up, and market.
- **No guarantee that a full rebuild is required.** If a focused refresh is enough, we will recommend that. You always get a written proposal before we build.
- **Timelines depend on content readiness.** Delays often come from waiting on photos, decisions, or approvals—not only from development time.
- **Submitting a contact form or booking a discovery call does not create a client engagement.** Paid work is governed by a separate written agreement.

## Scope boundaries

- We can write website copy based on what you tell us about the business. **We do not shoot photos or record video**; you provide images (or we advise on what works well on the site).
- We may use AI to assist development and drafting. Design, code, and copy are human-reviewed before they ship.
- Hosting, domain, third-party plugins, and stock assets may involve separate vendor fees unless included in writing.
- Ongoing maintenance, SEO campaigns, ads, and content publishing are separate services unless the agreement says otherwise.
